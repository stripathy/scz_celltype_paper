# Schizophrenia Literature Review: 5 Inhibitory Cell Types (Agent A — Sst, Pvalb, Vip, Lamp5, Sncg)

## Sst (Somatostatin interneurons)

**Top findings restated.** SST neurons show 345 BH-significant DE genes. Pathways: strong downregulation of HALLMARK_OXIDATIVE_PHOSPHORYLATION, REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM, and SRP-dependent cotranslational protein targeting. Top down genes include NAT16, SLC27A4, CCDC136, TRAPPC9, VAV2. Top up genes include SMAD1, GALNT13, DUBR, DCDC1, CEP126, plus an unexpected enrichment of male gamete / sex differentiation gene sets.

**What's been reported before.**
- SST mRNA reduction in DLPFC is one of the most replicated postmortem findings in SCZ, established by [Hashimoto et al. 2008, Cerebral Cortex](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/18/7/1575/419974), [Morris et al. 2008](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/18/7/1575/419974), and consolidated by the Lewis lab in [Fung et al. 2014, npj Schizophrenia](https://www.nature.com/articles/npjschz20144) and [Dienel/Glausier/Lewis 2023, JAMA Psychiatry](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2808723), which mapped the deficits to superficial-zone SST neurons specifically. The [Lewis lab's 2023 Cerebral Cortex paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10330559/) demonstrated markedly lower SST gene expression per neuron without missing neurons.
- Mitochondrial / OXPHOS dysfunction in SCZ has decades of bulk-tissue support ([Prabakaran et al. 2004; Ben-Shachar 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12072258/)) and is replicated at single-cell resolution by [Batiuk et al. 2022, Science Advances](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367), who reported energy-metabolism downregulation specifically in upper-layer GABAergic neurons. The [Ling/McCarroll 2024 Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5) showed coordinated neuron-astrocyte SNAP decline involving GABAergic neurons.
- Selenoprotein biology connects directly to interneuron oxidative defense: [Pitts et al. 2012, J Neurosci](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3362796/) showed selenoprotein P deletion impairs parvalbumin interneurons. Reduced selenium levels in SCZ blood/brain are supported by Mendelian randomization ([Liu et al. 2022, Front Nutrition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9445556/)).
- SMAD1 / BMP signaling is the surprise that aligns: [Bayes-Orive et al. 2024, Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07317-z) showed BMP2-SMAD1 controls PV interneuron glutamatergic innervation and perineuronal nets — and [Reble et al. 2022, J Neurosci Res](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jnr.25188) reported SMAD1/4/5/7 upregulation in SCZ brain and blood.

**Verdict per finding.**
- HALLMARK_OXIDATIVE_PHOSPHORYLATION down: **Known** (Prabakaran 2004; Batiuk 2022) — confirms in SST class, extends Batiuk's PV-centric finding.
- REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM down: **Partially known / extends prior work** — selenoprotein biology is implicated in interneuron pathology (Pitts 2012), but specifically observing selenoamino acid metabolism downregulation in SST class is novel.
- SRP_DEPENDENT_COTRANSLATIONAL_PROTEIN_TARGETING down: **Novel** in SCZ-SST context (based on keyword search, may need MeSH verification). Indirectly aligned with ribosomal/translation alterations from [Topol/Brennand 2015](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7769122/).
- SST as SCZ-vulnerable cell type: **Known** — Hashimoto 2008; Fung 2014; Dienel 2023.
- SMAD1 up: **Partially known** — Reble 2022 found SMAD1 upregulation in bulk SCZ; this work pinpoints SST and PV class specifically.
- NAT16, SLC27A4, CCDC136, TRAPPC9, VAV2, GALNT13, DUBR, DCDC1, CEP126: **Novel** at the gene level in SST class (based on keyword search). TRAPPC9 is a known intellectual-disability gene; SLC27A4 is a fatty-acid transporter with autism but not SCZ priors ([Maekawa 2015, Sci Rep](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep16239)).
- Male gamete / sex differentiation enrichment (up): **Novel** — likely an artefact of testis-shared transcripts in interneurons or a real signal worth Mendelian/MeSH verification.

**Coverage caveats.** Could not directly access the full-text of [Dienel 2023 JAMA Psychiatry](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2808723) or the Lewis lab "Alterations in Prefrontal Cortical Somatostatin Neurons" 2025 Biol Psychiatry paper (paywall, only abstract).

## Pvalb (Parvalbumin interneurons)

**Top findings restated.** 398 BH-significant DE genes. Strong downregulation of cytoplasmic ribosomal subunits (GOMF/GOCC ribosome) and SLIT/ROBO signaling. Top down genes: GALNT11, CHRD, MAN2C1, MIR9-3HG, EML1. Top up: SMAD1, SCN3A, FANCL, DCDC1, NDST4. Up pathways include male gamete generation and BMP-relevant TGF-beta receptor kinase activity.

**What's been reported before.**
- PV deficits in SCZ are a foundational finding (recently reviewed by [Marín 2024, Eur Neuropsychopharmacol / Mol Psychiatry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38490084/)). GAD67 and PV mRNA reductions are replicated across cohorts ([Lewis 2012, Trends Neurosci](https://www.cell.com/trends/neurosciences/fulltext/S0166-2236(11)00175-5)).
- PV mitochondrial / oxidative-stress vulnerability is the Do lab's central thesis ([Steullet/Do 2017, Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp201747)) and complemented by [Sahasrabuddhe et al. 2023 bioRxiv (cyclophilin D)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.05.19.541499.full.pdf) and [Khadimallah/Do 2022 Mol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9054672/) (miR137-COX6A2 biomarker).
- PV-specific single-cell SCZ findings: [Ruzicka et al. 2024, Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) (multi-cohort, ~140 donors) reports PV-expressing basket cells as the most genomically-converging inhibitory class. [Batiuk et al. 2022 Science Advances](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) found PV+ and CB+ subtypes with the largest compositional and transcriptomic changes in upper layers.
- PV-specific ribosomal/translational control: [Mahadevan et al. 2021, Mol Cell](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618116/) (ErbB4-TSC2 controls local translation at PV inputs) and [Khlghatyan/Beaulieu, Mol Psychiatry 2021 (FXR1)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01096-z) implicate translation machinery in PV-driven SCZ behaviors.
- SLIT/ROBO and interneuron migration: [Andrews et al. 2008, Dev Biol](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012160607015102) established SLIT-ROBO regulation of cortical interneuron migration; SLIT2/ROBO genes appear in cross-disorder psychiatric genetics ([Blockus & Chédotal 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959438814000531)).
- SMAD1 / BMP→ PV: [Bayes-Orive et al. 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07317-z) directly establishes BMP-SMAD1 signaling in PV interneurons — strongly aligned with our SMAD1 upregulation and TGF-beta receptor kinase pathway enrichment.

**Verdict per finding.**
- Ribosomal-subunit downregulation: **Partially known / extends prior work** — PV translation machinery is known to be specialized (Mahadevan 2021; FXR1), but global ribosomal-component loss in PV is a meta-analytic extension.
- SLIT-ROBO regulation downregulated: **Partially known** — SLIT/ROBO is established as an interneuron-migration system, but adult downregulation in PV in SCZ is a new specific finding.
- BMP/SMAD1 axis up: **Known/extends** — Bayes-Orive 2024 makes this immediately interpretable as a PV-specific BMP response; the cell-type assignment in our data tightly matches expectation.
- SMAD1 up: **Known** (Reble 2022 + Bayes-Orive 2024) — refines bulk-tissue findings to PV class.
- SCN3A up: **Novel-ish** — SCN3A (Nav1.3) is best known for early-onset epilepsy ([Helbig lab](https://www.helbiglab.io/blog/scn3a-a-sodium-channel-in-epilepsy-and-brain-malformations)) with SCZ links via SCN1A/SCN2A but not directly SCN3A; this may reflect SCZ-related excitability remodeling in PV cells (based on keyword search, may need MeSH verification).
- GALNT11, CHRD, MAN2C1, MIR9-3HG, EML1, FANCL, NDST4: **Novel** at the PV-cell-type level. Notably, CHRD encodes chordin, a BMP antagonist — its downregulation alongside SMAD1 upregulation is mechanistically coherent (loss of antagonist + active downstream signaling); this represents a novel candidate axis.
- Male gamete generation up: **Novel** — same caveat as for SST; likely a shared artefact of testis-brain co-expression but not previously emphasized in SCZ-PV literature.

**Coverage caveats.** Full text of Marín 2024 review only accessed via abstract. The [Khadimallah/Do 2022 Mol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9054672/) supplementary tables not exhaustively cross-referenced.

## Vip (VIP interneurons)

**Top findings restated.** 603 BH-significant DE genes (the highest among inhibitory classes). Top down genes: PRKCD, COL9A1, SLC27A4, HRH2, RGS8. Top up: RAB3IP, L3MBTL3, MXI1, NCOA2, DNAH6. Down pathways: organelle inner membrane, OXPHOS, phosphatidylcholine transporter. Up pathways: positive regulation of mRNA catabolism, REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP, negative regulation of gene expression (epigenetic).

**What's been reported before.**
- VIP-class deficits in SCZ are less consistently reported than SST/PV. [Volk/Lewis 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4133682/) noted PV/SST > VIP magnitude of alteration. However, recent snRNA-seq studies pulled VIP/5HT3aR neurons forward: [Ruzicka et al. 2024, Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) characterized VIP+ subtype changes; [Batiuk et al. 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) found upper-layer GABAergic shifts including VIP-class compositional changes.
- VIP interneuron developmental dysfunction impairing cortical circuits is a separate line: [Batista-Brito et al. 2017, bioRxiv → Neuron](https://escholarship.org/content/qt5v64w7zb/qt5v64w7zb.pdf).
- Mitochondrial inner-membrane dysfunction in SCZ: well-documented in bulk tissue ([Rajasekaran 2015, Neurosci Biobehav Rev](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7810242/); [Clay/Sillivan/Konradi 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3010320/)).
- BMP signaling and SCZ: same Bayes-Orive 2024 paper, but until now BMP was implicated in PV cells, not VIP. Our VIP-up BMP signaling result is a meaningful extension.
- PRKCD function in GABAergic neurons: PKCdelta is highly enriched in PV-CB1 interneurons and certain VIP-class cells across cortex/amygdala. Not previously implicated in SCZ specifically.
- RGS8: a Purkinje-/midbrain-enriched RGS family member; RGS4 is the canonical SCZ-associated RGS, RGS8 maps to the same 1q23.3-31 locus ([Campbell 2008, Pharmacogenomics J](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2486404)).

**Verdict per finding.**
- OXPHOS / organelle inner membrane down: **Known** — well-supported by bulk (Prabakaran 2004), iPSC (Brennand 2019; [Robicsek/Ben-Shachar 2019 Transl Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-019-0643-y)), and snRNA-seq (Batiuk 2022); extends to VIP class specifically.
- REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP up: **Partially known / extends prior work** — Bayes-Orive 2024 makes BMP central to PV biology; finding BMP-pathway upregulation in VIP cells too is a new cross-class observation worth follow-up.
- Negative regulation of gene expression (epigenetic) up: **Novel** in this class context (based on keyword search), but consistent with broader SCZ epigenomic dysregulation (Akbarian lab body of work).
- PRKCD down: **Novel** in SCZ-VIP (based on keyword search, may need MeSH verification). PKCdelta is known to be a major modulator of interneuron activity and fear learning.
- HRH2 (histamine H2 receptor) down: **Novel-ish** — histaminergic signaling implicated in SCZ via H3 antagonists; H2 downregulation in VIP is a novel observation.
- SLC27A4, COL9A1, RGS8: **Novel** at cell-type level (RGS8 partially aligned to RGS family/SCZ associations).
- RAB3IP, L3MBTL3, MXI1, NCOA2, DNAH6: **Novel.** MXI1 is a MYC repressor; NCOA2 is a nuclear-receptor coactivator (consistent with "negative regulation of gene expression" GSEA).

**Coverage caveats.** No SCZ-specific PRKCD postmortem study located; this gene-level finding is provisionally novel pending MeSH-verified queries.

## Lamp5 (LAMP5 interneurons)

**Top findings restated.** 400 BH-significant DE genes. Top down genes: SLC4A8, TLE2, PLD1, KCNQ4, ZNF75A. Top up: MGAT4A, MXI1, MAP4K3, ZNF516, CRBN. Down pathways: organelle inner membrane, vacuolar membrane, carbohydrate metabolism. Up pathways: HALLMARK_UV_RESPONSE_DN, embryonic axis specification, IL-12 production.

**What's been reported before.**
- LAMP5 interneurons are species-divergent (humans/primates >> mouse) and represent neurogliaform-like cells. The most comprehensive disease-context study is [Liguz-Lecznar/Pereira et al. 2022, Acta Neuropathologica](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00401-022-02457-w), showing LAMP5 interneuron loss in Alzheimer's disease drives network hyperexcitation and tau pathology accumulation.
- LAMP5 in SCZ specifically: cited as a layer marker in [Reiner/Crist single-cell-SCZ pilot work](https://www.med.upenn.edu/csa/assets/user-content/documents/Crist_10X_P30.pdf) and [Ruzicka 2024 Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136); LAMP5-class compositional reductions in SCZ are reported in [Batiuk 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) (upper-layer GABAergic loss includes neurogliaform/LAMP5 cells).
- Mitochondrial inner-membrane dysfunction: same OXPHOS literature applies (Prabakaran 2004; Rajasekaran 2015).
- CRBN (cereblon) in cognitive function: [Choi 2018 J Neurosci](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/38/14/3571) — CRBN regulates BK channels and synaptic function; CRBN deficits cause non-syndromic intellectual disability ([Higgins et al. 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11018632/)). Not classically SCZ.
- MXI1, MGAT4A, MAP4K3, KCNQ4, SLC4A8, PLD1, TLE2: no strong prior SCZ literature.

**Verdict per finding.**
- LAMP5 class involvement in SCZ: **Partially known / extends prior work** — Batiuk 2022 and Ruzicka 2024 placed LAMP5 in the broader picture; a 400-gene cell-type-specific DE signature is novel.
- Organelle inner-membrane (OXPHOS-related) down: **Known** — generalizes well-replicated SCZ OXPHOS findings to LAMP5 class.
- Vacuolar membrane / carbohydrate metabolism down: **Novel** in SCZ-LAMP5 (based on keyword search). Possibly related to autophagy / lysosomal dysfunction reports in SCZ (Schneider lab work).
- HALLMARK_UV_RESPONSE_DN up: **Novel** in SCZ-interneuron context. This gene set is largely a stress-/p53-response signature; its upregulation suggests a cellular stress response — interpretation should be made cautiously since this Hallmark is non-specific.
- Embryonic axis specification, IL-12 production up: **Novel** in this context; immune-related pathway up-regulation in LAMP5 aligns broadly with SCZ neuroinflammation literature ([Trépanier/Lipska 2016 Mol Psychiatry; Volk 2015](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2080683/)).
- SLC4A8, TLE2, PLD1, KCNQ4, ZNF75A, MGAT4A, MXI1, MAP4K3, ZNF516, CRBN: **Novel** at the gene-cell-type level (based on keyword search, may need MeSH verification). CRBN upregulation is intriguing given its E3-ligase role and the intellectual-disability connection.

**Coverage caveats.** Direct SCZ x LAMP5 literature is sparse (LAMP5 in SCZ has been studied mostly via composition, not gene-level DE); could not verify whether a 2024-2026 preprint specifically targets LAMP5 SCZ DE.

## Sncg (SNCG+ CCK-basket-like interneurons)

**Top findings restated.** Only 54 BH-significant DE genes — smallest signature among the five classes. Top down genes: RGS8, ATP1B3, CHRNA4, CHCHD6, USP31. Top up: C4orf33, NEK7, CRB1, FXR1, KIZ. Down pathways: REACTOME_EUKARYOTIC_TRANSLATION_ELONGATION / INITIATION, REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM. Up pathways: sex differentiation, immune receptor activity, signaling by BMP.

**What's been reported before.**
- Sncg-class interneurons correspond to CCK+ basket cells expressing CB1 cannabinoid receptor ([Tasic 2018, Bakken 2021 Allen Institute taxonomies](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/cellguide/CL_4023015); review: [Bartos & Elgueta](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11041731/)). They mediate depolarization-induced suppression of inhibition (DSI) via retrograde endocannabinoid signaling.
- SCZ-CCK-basket literature is small. [Volk/Lewis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4133682/) and the CB1-receptor SCZ postmortem literature (Eggan/Lewis 2008) provide indirect support — CB1 reductions on cortical axon terminals would functionally affect SNCG cells.
- Eukaryotic translation initiation / elongation deficits in psychiatric disorders: [Laguesse & Ron 2020 Trends Mol Med (eIF4E)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/genetics/articles/10.3389/fgene.2018.00561/full) and [Santini 2019, Cold Spring Harb Perspect Biol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7255087/) — translation machinery has been linked to mood disorders, autism, and SCZ via local dendritic synthesis.
- Selenoprotein/selenoamino acid metabolism: same Pitts 2012 paper applies; redirected here to SNCG class is novel.
- FXR1 up: [Khlghatyan/Beaulieu 2018, Front Mol Neurosci; 2021 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01096-z) — FXR1 in PV interneurons is implicated in SCZ; upregulation in SNCG class is new.
- CHRNA4 (nAChR alpha-4 subunit) SCZ associations: [Higashi et al. 2008 Schizophr Res](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18762859/); CHRNA4 SNPs associated with prefrontal attention endophenotypes and smoking comorbidity.
- RGS8: shares 1q23 locus with RGS2/4/5/16 (a SCZ susceptibility region; [Campbell 2008](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2486404)).
- BMP/SMAD up: same Bayes-Orive 2024 framework.

**Verdict per finding.**
- REACTOME_EUKARYOTIC_TRANSLATION_ELONGATION and INITIATION down: **Partially known / extends prior work** — translation dysregulation is implicated broadly in SCZ but not previously pinpointed to SNCG class.
- REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM down: same as SST — **Partially known**, extends Pitts 2012 selenoprotein-interneuron axis to SNCG cells.
- CHRNA4 down: **Known** — CHRNA4 has SCZ genetic and endophenotype associations (Higashi 2008); cell-type-specific downregulation in SNCG class is a new functional layer on existing genetic priors.
- RGS8 down: **Partially known / extends prior work** — RGS family SCZ associations (RGS4 canonical; RGS8 by chromosomal proximity); first cell-type-resolved downregulation in SCZ.
- FXR1 up: **Partially known / extends prior work** — FXR1 PV-interneuron deficits are linked to SCZ-like behaviors (Khlghatyan 2021); SNCG-class upregulation suggests cell-type-divergent regulation.
- REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP up: as above — **Partially known**, extends Bayes-Orive 2024 to SNCG.
- GOMF_IMMUNE_RECEPTOR_ACTIVITY up: **Novel** in this class. Consistent with broader SCZ neuroinflammation findings.
- Sex differentiation up: **Novel** — recurring across SST/Sncg, possibly shared confound or biology.
- ATP1B3, CHCHD6, USP31, C4orf33, NEK7, CRB1, KIZ: **Novel** at gene level (based on keyword search). NEK7 has an inflammasome role; CRB1 is a polarity protein primarily known in retina.

**Coverage caveats.** SNCG/CCK-basket-cell SCZ literature is the sparsest of the five classes; absence of prior reports may reflect attention bias rather than absence of biology. Could not access the [Bartos & Elgueta PV-CCK 2024 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11041731/) in full.

### Cross-cutting observations from this agent
- **Recurrent OXPHOS / mitochondrial / inner-membrane downregulation** across SST, Vip, and Lamp5 cells is consistent with the deep literature on SCZ mitochondrial dysfunction (Prabakaran 2004; Brennand iPSC; Batiuk 2022; Ling 2024).
- **Recurrent SMAD1 / BMP-signaling upregulation** across SST, Pvalb, Vip, Sncg fits the new Bayes-Orive 2024 framework where BMP-SMAD1 governs PV (and likely other GABAergic) excitatory-input regulation; this is the strongest cross-class biological story this dataset confirms.
- **Recurrent sex differentiation / male gamete gene-set enrichment** across SST, Pvalb, Sncg is most likely an artefact of testis/brain-shared transcripts (e.g., GTEx-known phenomenon) — recommend re-running GSEA after removing testis-restricted leading-edge genes before reporting this as a biological finding.
- **Translation-machinery downregulation** (ribosomal subunits in Pvalb; SRP in Sst; EIF/translation pathways in Sncg) is consistent with the FXR1 / ErbB4-TSC2 / eIF literature implicating local protein synthesis in PV and broader inhibitory neuron pathology.
- Top genes such as NAT16, GALNT11, MGAT4A, ZNF516, NDST4, KIZ, CCDC136 have minimal prior SCZ literature and warrant orthogonal validation before being highlighted.

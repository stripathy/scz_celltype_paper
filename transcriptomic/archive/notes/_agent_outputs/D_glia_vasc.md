# SCZ Glial and Vascular Literature Review (Agent D — Astro, Oligo, OPC, Micro-PVM, Endo, VLMC)

## Astro

**Top findings restated.** Strong UP-regulation of interferon-gamma/alpha response (NES = 2.73, 2.88) and broader cytokine signalling; concurrent DOWN-regulation of trans-synaptic signalling and ion transmembrane transport. Top UP genes include FOXP1, SP110 (an interferon-induced PML body protein), RUFY4, SPTLC3, HMGCLL1. Top DOWN genes include D2HGDH, PYGM, CASKIN1, LRRC4B.

**What's been reported before.** A large body of work supports astrocyte dysfunction in SCZ, but with mixed signs. [Webster et al. 2005](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S096999610500241X) and [Steffek et al. 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12223243/) found *reductions* in GFAP in DLPFC layer V, contradicting any simple "astrogliosis" model. Conversely, [Catts et al. 2014](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0004867414531078) found increased astrocyte markers in an inflammation-positive SCZ subgroup, and [van Kesteren et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28350400/) meta-analyzed postmortem evidence for pro-inflammatory molecular changes. Single-cell era: [Batiuk et al. 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) identified five cortical astrocyte subtypes and SCZ-associated perturbations; [Ling et al. 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5) found the Synaptic Neuron-and-Astrocyte Program (SNAP) declines in both ageing and SCZ — directly compatible with your synaptic-membrane DOWN signature. [Ruzicka et al. 2024 Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) reported astrocyte DEGs across 25 cell types in a 140-donor cohort, and [Bast et al. 2025 medRxiv (PREPRINT)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40162239/) found pronounced mitochondrial signatures (with neurons most affected). [Akkouh et al. 2022 (iPSC astroglia)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8556332/) showed divergent transcriptomic patterns in SCZ astroglia, including calcium and glutamate-uptake deficits.

**Special focus — IFN-gamma signature.** This is the most striking finding. Cortical IFN-gamma activation has been hypothesized in SCZ — [Watanabe 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9075278/) reported altered IFN-gamma production in SCZ families, and SCZ is HLA-associated with the MHC region carrying the strongest GWAS signal. Mechanistically, [Warre-Cornish et al. 2020 Sci Adv](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aay9506) showed IFN-gamma exposure of iPSC neurons reproduces SCZ-relevant MHCI/synapsin phenotypes — but reactive **astrocytes** with IFN signatures (IRRAs; [Maciel-Barón et al. 2024 Glia](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/glia.24494)) have been characterized mostly in MS/AD contexts. SP110 induction — your top UP hit and a known interferon-inducible PML body protein — is mechanistically consistent with the Warre-Cornish iPSC PML/IFN-gamma pathway and is, to our knowledge, **novel as a postmortem SCZ astrocyte finding**.

**Verdict per finding.** Synaptic-membrane DOWN: **Known/Partial** (Webster, Ling/SNAP). Cytokine/IFN UP: **Partial-to-Novel** — astrocyte-specific IFN-gamma signatures in postmortem cortical SCZ are emerging (Catts subgroup; Bast preprint) but the magnitude here (NES ≈ 2.7-2.9) is unusually strong; SP110 specifically is novel. FOXP1 UP is consistent with [Reber et al. 2025](https://academic.oup.com/hmg/article/35/2/ddaf180/8362150) FOXP1-regulated SCZ risk.

**Coverage caveats.** SCZ astrocyte inflammation is highly heterogeneous between cohorts ("high-inflammation" subgroups, Fillman et al.); meta-analyzing 7 cohorts may have averaged across this heterogeneity. Antipsychotic exposure remains an uncontrolled confound for SREBP/lipid genes.

## Oligo

**Top findings restated.** Synaptic signalling DOWN (NES = -2.32, padj = 2.4e-21 — your strongest signal); synaptic membrane and axonal genes DOWN; xenobiotic/biological-oxidations UP. Top DOWN genes: KCNA2, PLD5, CNDP1, KIT, CTNNA2 — note carnosine-dipeptidase CNDP1 is highly oligodendrocyte-enriched.

**What's been reported before.** Oligodendrocyte and myelin downregulation is one of the most-replicated postmortem SCZ findings, beginning with [Hakak et al. 2001 PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3622281/) showing MAG, MAL, CNP, HER3, gelsolin, transferrin all DOWN in DLPFC, replicated by [Tkachev et al. 2003 Lancet](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140673603142894/fulltext) (3.3-fold MAG reduction). Aberg et al. 2006 identified QKI as a regulator of myelin genes, and QKI subsequently shown to cooperate with [SREBP2 in cholesterol biosynthesis](https://elifesciences.org/articles/60467) — directly relevant to your cholesterol-biosynthesis DOWN signal. [Iwamoto et al. SOX10 methylation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6725011/) provides an epigenetic mechanism. snRNA-seq era: [Ruzicka 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) and the [npj Schizophrenia 2025 GWAS-snRNA integration](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1566155/full) further confirm oligodendrocyte involvement. [Trubetskoy 2022 PGC3 GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04434-5) showed enrichment in oligodendrocyte and OPC gene sets. The [2025 Translational Psychiatry iPSC paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-025-03509-x) extends findings to differentiation/morphology.

**Special focus — synaptic signalling DOWN in oligos.** This is biologically unusual: classical oligodendrocytes don't form synapses (OPCs do — see [Bergles et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-022-01777-3)). Three interpretations: (1) **myelin-supported axonal genes** picked up as "synaptic" by GO annotation — many synaptic-membrane components (e.g. KCNA2, a juxtaparanodal K+ channel) are expressed at axon-glial junctions; KCNA2 specifically clusters at the paranode and is critical for axon-myelin function. (2) **contamination/transcript leakage** from adjacent myelinated axons via mRNA in myelin — but this would be a non-disease confound. (3) **genuine reduction in OL-axon coupling** affecting expression of OL-side ion-channel/cell-adhesion machinery; this aligns with the dysfunctional macro-connectivity model. This specific "synaptic-signalling DOWN in mature oligodendrocytes" finding has not, to our knowledge, been highlighted as a coherent pathway-level signal in prior SCZ work — partial novelty here, though the constituent myelin/axon-coupling genes are well-known.

**Cholesterol biosynthesis DOWN.** Consistent with [QKI/SREBP2 axis (Zhou et al.)](https://elifesciences.org/articles/60467) and [SREBF1/2 GWAS associations (Steen et al.)](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp2008110). The opposite OPC direction (UP) is intriguing — compensation/proliferation hypothesis.

**Verdict per finding.** Myelin/axon DOWN: **Known** (Hakak, Tkachev). Cholesterol DOWN: **Known/Partial** (QKI/SREBP literature predicts it; direct demonstration in SCZ snRNA-seq is recent). Xenobiotic/biological-oxidations UP: **Novel** in SCZ — typically discussed in [redox dysregulation (Steullet et al.)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5887379/) but rarely as a discrete pathway hit.

**Coverage caveats.** Oligodendrocyte contamination by axonal mRNA is hard to rule out in snRNA-seq. Antipsychotics modulate SREBP/cholesterol pathways ([Polymeropoulos et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1635424/)), an unresolved confound.

## OPC

**Top findings restated.** Trans-synaptic signalling and cell-projection-morphogenesis DOWN; HALLMARK_MTORC1_SIGNALING strongly UP (NES = 2.37), with translation initiation UP — and cholesterol-biosynthesis trending opposite to mature Oligo (UP, possibly compensatory). Top UP: SLC26A2, VWA5A, HK2, AZGP1, CSF1; top DOWN: PREX2, MIR9-3HG, NKAIN3, ITGB5, CASK.

**What's been reported before.** OPC involvement in SCZ has been established but not as deeply phenotyped as mature oligos. [Mauney et al. 2015 Schizophr Res](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4681621/) showed OLIG2+ cell density decreased while NG2+ (OPC) density was unaltered — implying impaired differentiation rather than loss. Earlier, [Mauney et al. 2013](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01372-y) showed PNN loss in SCZ that may implicate OPC-derived ECM. [Crawford and colleagues](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cellular-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncel.2016.00078/full) found decreased oligodendrocyte numbers in hippocampal subfields linked to cognitive deficits, replicated by [Falkai's group (2018)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00406-018-0888-0). [Bergles' pathological OPCs in SCZ brains 2022 Mol Psych](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-022-01777-3) showed OPC-synaptic connectivity dysfunction triggering SCZ-like behaviors. [Frontiers Psychiatry 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1566155/full) re-confirmed OPCs as the most genetic-risk-enriched white-matter cell type in SCZ.

**Special focus — mTORC1 UP.** [Frontiers Pharmacology 2020 (Ibarra-Lecue et al.)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7105616/) found ribosomal S6 hypofunction and *downregulated* AKT-mTORC1 protein signaling in postmortem SCZ DLPFC — appearing to **contradict** your finding. But that study was bulk tissue, dominated by neurons; an OPC-specific UP could coexist with neuronal downregulation. mTORC1 is a master regulator of OPC proliferation. Antipsychotics [activate mTORC1 (Bowling et al. Sci Signal)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scisignal.2004331) — a possible non-disease driver. HK2 UP (glycolysis) is consistent with a stress/proliferation OPC state; CSF1 UP suggests OPC-microglia crosstalk. **Net: a coherent "stressed/compensatory-proliferative OPC" signature, partially novel as a unified pathway-level SCZ finding.**

**Verdict per finding.** Synaptic-signalling DOWN in OPCs: **Partial** (consistent with Bergles 2022 synaptic-defect findings). mTORC1 UP: **Partial-to-Novel** — opposite-direction to bulk tissue mTORC1 reports, but mechanistically consistent with OPC proliferation/stress biology; possibly novel as a specific snRNA-seq finding. Cholesterol UP (vs Oligo DOWN): **Novel** as a within-lineage divergence; compatible with compensatory OPC proliferation.

**Coverage caveats.** OPCs are sparse — your n_sig = 259 with 10,040 tested may still be underpowered relative to neurons. Distinguishing genuinely activated mTORC1 from antipsychotic-driven activation requires drug-free or first-episode replication.

## Micro-PVM

**Top findings restated.** Massively UP translation/ribosome pathways (REACTOME_TRANSLATION NES = 3.17, padj = 1.2e-14; cytoplasmic translation NES = 2.96; translation initiation NES = 3.08) — the strongest signal in the whole dataset. Top UP: IGF2R, PSTPIP2, HPRT1, FCGR1A (Fc-gamma receptor), SNAP23. Top DOWN: VASH1, ZFP36L2, HGSNAT, RNF19B, KCNQ1. GPCR signalling and motility DOWN.

**What's been reported before.** Microglia in SCZ is contentious. [van Kesteren et al. 2017 Transl Psych](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28350400/) meta-analyzed 41 postmortem studies (783 SCZ vs 762 controls) and reported *increased* microglial density and pro-inflammatory expression. [Marques et al. 2019 Psychol Med](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6366560/) meta-analyzed in-vivo TSPO PET and found increased BP but no VT change — methodology-dependent. [Snijders et al. 2021 Glia](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002%2Fglia.23962) — pivotal — reported *loss* of mature/homeostatic microglial markers (CX3CR1, CSF1R, TMEM119, IRF8) **without** immune activation. [Schnieder et al.](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpsy/article/PIIS2215-0366(17)30101-3/abstract) and [Gober et al. 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/bpa.13003) describe region-specific morphological changes. [Lago et al. 2023 Transl Psych](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-023-02677-y) mapped a "core" SCZ microglia transcriptional signature. [Sekar et al. 2016 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0334-7) (C4 study; supportive functional follow-up by [Sellgren et al. 2019 Nat Neurosci](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0334-7)) established microglia-mediated synaptic pruning as a SCZ mechanism. Genetic enrichment is *weak* for microglia in SCZ ([Skene/PGC](https://academic.oup.com/schizophreniabulletin/article/40/4/925/1901106)), unlike Alzheimer's where microglia dominate genetic risk.

**Special focus — translation UP.** A massive ribosomal/translation upregulation is a classical **activated-microglia** transcriptional signature seen in LPS challenge models, AD, and aging microglia. [The mTOR-4E-BP1 axis controls microglia inflammatory responses](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11709613/), and [LPS-treated microglia upregulate translation/ribosome biogenesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7064956/). Has this been reported in **SCZ specifically**? Less commonly — most SCZ microglia work emphasizes either density (Van Kesteren) or homeostatic loss (Snijders). PSTPIP2 in microglia marks autoinflammatory states; FCGR1A is a phagocytic/activation marker — together these support an *activated* phenotype. This **contrasts** with Snijders 2021's loss-of-homeostatic-markers-without-activation finding, suggesting your 7-cohort meta-analysis may resolve true activation that smaller cohorts missed, or may reflect dying-cell/protein-synthesis stress.

**Verdict per finding.** Translation UP: **Partial-to-Novel** for SCZ specifically — consistent with the broader microglia activation literature but a discrete pathway-level finding in SCZ snRNA-seq has not been emphasized. Activation markers (PSTPIP2, FCGR1A): **Partial** — consistent with van Kesteren's pro-inflammatory signal. GPCR/motility DOWN: aligns with loss of homeostatic surveillance (Snijders).

**Coverage caveats.** Microglia ex vivo profiles are heavily distorted by tissue processing — snRNA-seq mitigates but does not eliminate this. PMI/RIN confounds and antipsychotic effects on microglial activation states are real concerns.

## Endo

**Top findings restated.** Neuron/axon-development pathways DOWN; ribosome/translation initiation UP (smaller effect than microglia). Top DOWN: GPCPD1, PLPPR4, TNFSF10 (TRAIL), SLCO1A2 (transporter), ABCG2 (efflux transporter). Top UP: TNFRSF1A (TNF receptor), KANSL1L, SULF2, PIBF1, CFH (complement factor H).

**What's been reported before.** BBB/endothelial involvement in SCZ has emerged as a defined research area. [Najjar et al. 2017 Front Psychiatry](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2017.00083/full) provided the foundational neurovascular-unit/BBB hyperpermeability hypothesis. [Kealy et al. 2018 Mol Psych](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28993710/) showed claudin-5 dose-dependent expression links 22q11DS BBB compromise to SCZ; [Greene et al. 2020 Transl Psych](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-020-01054-3) extended tight-junction findings across major psychiatric disorders. [Harris et al. 2008 PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0003964) used laser-capture microvasculature transcriptomics and found a "hypo-inflammatory" endothelial state with altered inflammatory genes. [Cai et al. 2019 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-018-0235-x) reported increased macrophages and altered endothelial gene expression in the high-inflammation SCZ subgroup (notably elevated CDH5, OCLN; decreased **ABCG2** — matching your direction). [Puvogel et al. 2022 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-022-01796-0) did midbrain BBB snRNA-seq finding subtle changes (FOXP2, PDE4D) with preserved proportions. [Schizophrenia organoid endothelial study (PMC10806043)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10806043/) showed higher permeability and altered angiogenesis. [BBB Disruption review 2025 MDPI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11817713/) consolidates BBB dysfunction as a key pathophysiological factor.

**Verdict per finding.** ABCG2 DOWN: **Known** — directly replicates [Cai et al. 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-018-0235-x). SLCO1A2 DOWN (organic anion transporter): **Novel** in SCZ but consistent with BBB transport compromise. TNF receptor (TNFRSF1A) UP / TRAIL (TNFSF10) DOWN: **Novel** at gene level; consistent with the broader pro-inflammatory endothelial picture. Neuron/axon-development DOWN: **Novel** — though may reflect contamination of endothelial nuclei with neuronal RNA, or genuine endothelial-derived axonal guidance signals (e.g. semaphorins). Complement factor H UP: complements ([Sekar et al. 2016 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0334-7)) the broader complement-in-SCZ story.

**Coverage caveats.** Endothelial cells are sparse in snRNA-seq (n_sig 139/4473 tested); meta-analyzing 7 cohorts is essential to reach this power. Region matters (Puvogel midbrain vs your cortical) and may explain discrepancies in direction.

## VLMC

**Top findings restated.** Strong DOWN of monoatomic ion-transmembrane-transporter activity (NES = -2.29, -2.12, -2.22 across three GO sets); ribosome UP. Top DOWN: RERGL, PTN (pleiotrophin), SEMA5A, KCNA2, ARHGAP29. Top UP: P4HA1 (collagen prolyl hydroxylase), XPO1, KANSL1L, MKLN1, CERS6.

**What's been reported before.** VLMC literature in SCZ is **extremely sparse** — VLMCs were first identified by scRNA-seq only in 2018 ([Vanlandewijck et al. Nature](https://trailbio.com/vascular-leptomeningeal-cells-vlmcs-blood-brain-barrier-function/)). Most SCZ-relevant findings come tangentially via BBB and meninges studies. [DeCarvalho et al. 2023 Nature Comms](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42825-y) and the [BioRxiv human leptomeninges single-cell atlas (PREPRINT)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.12.15.520647.full.pdf) catalogued human meningeal fibroblast subtypes. [The Sun et al. 2023 mouse leptomeninges paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10515796/) directly noted *"unexpected association of meningeal fibroblast and endothelial gene expression with mental illness, particularly schizophrenia"* and showed regional/age fibroblast heterogeneity implicated in mental disorders. [Puvogel et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-022-01796-0) included fibroblasts in their midbrain BBB snRNA-seq cohort. P4HA1 UP (collagen biosynthesis) is mechanistically consistent with extracellular-matrix and vasculature dysregulation in inflammation-positive SCZ. PTN DOWN is interesting — PTN/PTPRZ signalling has been linked to SCZ.

**Verdict per finding.** Ion-transmembrane DOWN: **Novel** for VLMC in SCZ specifically — VLMCs aren't typically discussed as ion-transport cells, so this could indicate altered ECM-secretion machinery rather than channel function per se. Ribosome UP: **Novel**. P4HA1 UP: **Partial** — fits ECM remodeling story (Sun mouse meninges). PTN DOWN: **Partial** — consistent with PTN as a SCZ-relevant trophic factor.

**Coverage caveats.** VLMC literature in SCZ is essentially absent — n_sig of 40/1678 in your meta-analysis is itself one of the largest VLMC datasets in SCZ. Direction-of-effect claims should be tentative. The lack of prior work makes both replication and contradiction impossible; interpret findings as hypothesis-generating.

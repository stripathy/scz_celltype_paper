## Inhibitory neurons (well-studied)

### Sst (Somatostatin interneurons)

**Top findings.** 345 BH-significant DE genes. Strong DOWN of HALLMARK_OXIDATIVE_PHOSPHORYLATION (NES = -2.14, padj = 3e-5), REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM, SRP-dependent cotranslational protein targeting. Top DOWN genes: NAT16, SLC27A4, CCDC136, TRAPPC9, VAV2. Top UP: SMAD1, GALNT13, DUBR, DCDC1, CEP126.

**Prior literature.**
- SST mRNA reduction in DLPFC is one of the most-replicated SCZ findings: [Hashimoto et al. 2008, Cerebral Cortex](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/18/7/1575/419974); [Morris et al. 2008](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/18/7/1575/419974); [Fung et al. 2014, npj Schizophrenia](https://www.nature.com/articles/npjschz20144); [Dienel/Glausier/Lewis 2023, JAMA Psychiatry](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2808723) (SCZ-specific, more severe than PV).
- Mitochondrial / OXPHOS dysfunction in SCZ: foundational [Prabakaran 2004; Ben-Shachar 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12072258/); single-cell [Batiuk et al. 2022, Sci Adv](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) (energy-metabolism DOWN in upper-layer GABAergic); [Ling/McCarroll 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5) (SNAP decline); iPSC [Ni et al. 2019 Mol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6813882/) (mixed PV+SST CIN OxPhos deficit — **the most directly relevant prior result**).
- Selenoprotein biology and interneurons: [Pitts et al. 2012, J Neurosci](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3362796/); reduced selenium in SCZ blood/brain — [Liu et al. 2022, Front Nutrition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9445556/) (Mendelian randomization).
- SMAD1 / BMP: [Bayes-Orive et al. 2024, Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07317-z) (BMP2-SMAD1 controls PV interneuron glutamatergic innervation); [Reble et al. 2022, J Neurosci Res](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jnr.25188) (SMAD1/4/5/7 UP in SCZ brain and blood).

**Verdicts.**
- HALLMARK_OXIDATIVE_PHOSPHORYLATION ↓: **Known** (Prabakaran, Batiuk, Ni) — extends Ni 2019 to postmortem adult brain with cell-type resolution.
- REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM ↓: **Partial** — extends Pitts 2012 selenoprotein-interneuron axis.
- SRP_DEPENDENT_COTRANSLATIONAL_PROTEIN_TARGETING ↓: **Novel** (keyword-search caveat).
- SST as SCZ-vulnerable cell type: **Known** (Hashimoto, Fung, Dienel).
- SMAD1 ↑: **Partial** (Reble 2022 bulk; this work pinpoints SST class).
- NAT16, SLC27A4, CCDC136, TRAPPC9, VAV2, GALNT13, DUBR, DCDC1, CEP126: **Novel** at gene-cell-type level.
- Male-gamete UP gene sets: likely **GSEA artefact** (testis-shared transcripts in interneurons).

**Coverage caveats.** Dienel 2023 JAMA Psychiatry and Lewis 2025 Biol Psych SST paper full-text not accessible.

### Pvalb (Parvalbumin interneurons)

**Top findings.** 398 BH-significant. Strong DOWN of cytoplasmic ribosomal subunits and SLIT/ROBO signaling. Top DOWN: GALNT11, CHRD, MAN2C1, MIR9-3HG, EML1. Top UP: SMAD1, SCN3A, FANCL, DCDC1, NDST4.

**Prior literature.**
- PV deficits in SCZ: reviewed in [Marín 2024 Eur Neuropsychopharmacol](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38490084/); GAD67/PV reductions replicated in [Lewis 2012, Trends Neurosci](https://www.cell.com/trends/neurosciences/fulltext/S0166-2236(11)00175-5).
- PV mitochondrial / oxidative-stress vulnerability: Do lab [Steullet et al. 2017, Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp201747); [Sahasrabuddhe 2023 bioRxiv (PREPRINT)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.05.19.541499.full.pdf); [Khadimallah/Do 2022 Mol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9054672/) (miR137-COX6A2).
- PV-specific snRNA-seq SCZ: [Ruzicka et al. 2024, Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136); [Batiuk et al. 2022 Sci Adv](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367).
- PV translation machinery: [Mahadevan et al. 2021, Mol Cell](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618116/) (ErbB4-TSC2); [Khlghatyan/Beaulieu 2021 Mol Psychiatry FXR1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01096-z).
- SLIT/ROBO and interneuron migration: [Andrews et al. 2008, Dev Biol](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012160607015102); [Blockus & Chédotal 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959438814000531).
- SMAD1 / BMP → PV: [Bayes-Orive 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07317-z) (BMP-SMAD1 directly in PV).

**Verdicts.**
- Ribosomal subunit ↓: **Partial** — extends PV translation specialization (Mahadevan 2021, FXR1).
- SLIT-ROBO ↓: **Partial** — known migration role; adult downregulation in PV in SCZ is new.
- BMP/SMAD1 ↑: **Known** (Bayes-Orive 2024 makes this expected for PV).
- SMAD1 ↑: **Known** (Reble 2022 + Bayes-Orive 2024).
- SCN3A ↑: **Novel-ish** — SCN3A (Nav1.3) not directly in SCZ.
- **CHRD ↓ + SMAD1 ↑ axis**: mechanistically coherent — loss of BMP antagonist + active downstream signaling. Novel observation.
- GALNT11, MAN2C1, MIR9-3HG, EML1, FANCL, NDST4: **Novel** at PV-cell-type level.

**Coverage caveats.** Marín 2024 abstract only; Khadimallah 2022 supplementary tables not cross-referenced.

### Vip (VIP interneurons)

**Top findings.** 603 BH-significant (highest among inhibitory). Top DOWN: PRKCD, COL9A1, SLC27A4, HRH2, RGS8. Top UP: RAB3IP, L3MBTL3, MXI1, NCOA2, DNAH6. Top DOWN pathways: organelle inner membrane, OXPHOS, phosphatidylcholine transporter. Top UP pathways: positive regulation of mRNA catabolism, REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP, negative regulation of gene expression (epigenetic).

**Prior literature.**
- VIP-class deficits are less consistently reported than SST/PV ([Volk/Lewis 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4133682/) noted PV/SST > VIP magnitude). Recent snRNA-seq has pulled VIP forward: [Ruzicka 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136), [Batiuk 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367).
- VIP interneuron developmental dysfunction: [Batista-Brito et al. 2017](https://escholarship.org/content/qt5v64w7zb/qt5v64w7zb.pdf).
- Mitochondrial inner-membrane dysfunction in SCZ: [Rajasekaran 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7810242/); [Clay/Sillivan/Konradi 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3010320/).
- BMP and SCZ: [Bayes-Orive 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07317-z) (BMP in PV) — our VIP-up BMP signal extends this cross-class.
- RGS family: [Campbell 2008, Pharmacogenomics J](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2486404) (RGS4 canonical; RGS8 in same 1q23 locus).

**Verdicts.**
- OXPHOS / organelle inner-membrane ↓: **Known** (Prabakaran, [Robicsek/Ben-Shachar 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-019-0643-y), Batiuk 2022) extends to VIP.
- REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP ↑: **Partial** — extends Bayes-Orive 2024 (PV) to VIP.
- Negative regulation of gene expression (epigenetic) ↑: **Novel** at class level; consistent with Akbarian SCZ epigenomic work.
- PRKCD ↓: **Novel** in SCZ-VIP.
- HRH2 ↓: **Novel-ish** (H3 implicated in SCZ pharmacology; H2 novel).
- RAB3IP, L3MBTL3, MXI1 (MYC repressor), NCOA2, DNAH6: **Novel.**

**Coverage caveats.** No PRKCD-specific SCZ postmortem study located.

### Lamp5 (LAMP5 interneurons)

**Top findings.** 400 BH-significant. Top DOWN: SLC4A8, TLE2, PLD1, KCNQ4, ZNF75A. Top UP: MGAT4A, MXI1, MAP4K3, ZNF516, CRBN. Top DOWN pathways: organelle inner membrane, vacuolar membrane, carbohydrate metabolism. Top UP: HALLMARK_UV_RESPONSE_DN, embryonic axis specification, IL-12 production.

**Prior literature.**
- LAMP5 interneurons are species-divergent (humans/primates >> mouse). Disease context: [Liguz-Lecznar/Pereira et al. 2022, Acta Neuropathol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00401-022-02457-w) (LAMP5 loss in AD drives hyperexcitation).
- LAMP5 in SCZ: cited in [Ruzicka 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136); [Batiuk 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) (compositional reductions).
- CRBN (cereblon): [Choi 2018 J Neurosci](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/38/14/3571) (regulates BK channels); CRBN deficits cause intellectual disability.

**Verdicts.**
- LAMP5 class involvement: **Partial** (Batiuk, Ruzicka cite class; 400-gene signature novel).
- Organelle inner-membrane ↓: **Known** (generalizes OXPHOS to LAMP5).
- Vacuolar membrane / carbohydrate metabolism ↓: **Novel** (possible autophagy/lysosomal angle).
- HALLMARK_UV_RESPONSE_DN ↑ (p53/stress): **Novel**; non-specific Hallmark.
- IL-12 production ↑: **Novel** in LAMP5; aligns with broader SCZ neuroinflammation.
- SLC4A8, TLE2, PLD1, KCNQ4, ZNF75A, MGAT4A, MXI1, MAP4K3, ZNF516, CRBN: **Novel.**

### Sncg (SNCG+/CCK-basket-like interneurons)

**Top findings.** 54 BH-significant (smallest signature). Top DOWN: RGS8, ATP1B3, CHRNA4, CHCHD6, USP31. Top UP: C4orf33, NEK7, CRB1, FXR1, KIZ. Top DOWN pathways: REACTOME_EUKARYOTIC_TRANSLATION_ELONGATION/INITIATION, REACTOME_SELENOAMINO_ACID_METABOLISM. Top UP: sex differentiation, immune receptor activity, REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP.

**Prior literature.**
- Sncg-class = CCK+ basket cells with CB1 cannabinoid receptor; mediate DSI ([Bartos & Elgueta](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11041731/)).
- CCK-basket SCZ literature small; indirect support via CB1 reductions ([Eggan/Lewis 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4133682/)).
- Translation initiation / elongation in psychiatric disease: [Laguesse & Ron 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/genetics/articles/10.3389/fgene.2018.00561/full); [Santini 2019 CSHB Perspect Biol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7255087/).
- FXR1 in PV interneurons: [Khlghatyan/Beaulieu 2021 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01096-z); SNCG-class upregulation new.
- CHRNA4: [Higashi 2008 Schizophr Res](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18762859/) (SNPs with prefrontal attention endophenotypes, smoking).
- RGS8: 1q23 locus ([Campbell 2008](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2486404)).

**Verdicts.**
- Translation elongation/initiation ↓: **Partial** — translation dysregulation known; SNCG-class novel.
- Selenoamino acid metabolism ↓: **Partial** (extends Pitts 2012 to SNCG).
- CHRNA4 ↓: **Known** (SCZ genetic/endophenotype priors); novel cell-type localization.
- RGS8 ↓: **Partial** (RGS family SCZ priors).
- FXR1 ↑: **Partial** (Khlghatyan 2021); divergent cell-type direction.
- REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP ↑: **Partial** (extends Bayes-Orive to SNCG).
- Immune receptor activity ↑: **Novel** at class.
- ATP1B3, CHCHD6, USP31, C4orf33, NEK7, CRB1, KIZ: **Novel.**

## Inhibitory neurons (under-studied subclasses) + rare excitatory

### Chandelier (axo-axonic PV interneuron)

**Top findings.** Top DOWN: FBLN7, KCNC1, VGF, MICAL2, KLHL5. Top UP: ADCY10, LRRTM2, EIF2S2, MBOAT2, ZNF680. Top DOWN pathways: GOCC_SYNAPTIC_MEMBRANE (padj=0.00016), GOCC_POSTSYNAPTIC_MEMBRANE, GOMF_GATED_CHANNEL_ACTIVITY. Top UP: HALLMARK_MTORC1_SIGNALING (padj=0.00016), REACTOME_DOWNSTREAM_TCR_SIGNALING, HALLMARK_MYC_TARGETS_V1.

**Prior literature.**
- Lewis-lab canonical findings: reduced GAT-1 axon cartridges innervating AIS with reciprocal GABA-A α2 up. [Woo et al. 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9560277/); [Pierri et al. 1999](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/ajp.156.11.1709); [Volk et al. 2002](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/12/10/1063/268401); [Cruz et al. 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19322171/); reviews [Lewis 2011](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/dneu.20825); [Rocco et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5374057/).
- Kv channels in PV: [Georgiev et al. 2014](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.2013.13040468) KCNS3 ↓; KCNC1 (Kv3.1) reduced in untreated SCZ DLPFC and normalized by antipsychotics ([review MDPI 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/25/16/8696)).
- VGF: cross-disorder neuropeptide reduction, downstream of DISC1 ([Thakker-Varia & Alder 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4204764/); [Brain Comms 2021](https://academic.oup.com/braincomms/article/3/4/fcab261/6412560)).
- mTORC1/AKT in SCZ DLPFC: bulk reports show downregulation ([Chadha & Meador-Woodruff 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7162985/); [Ibarra-Lecue et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105616/)).
- LRRTM family: LRRTM1 SCZ risk variants ([Wen et al. 2021 Mol Psych](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-021-01146-6)); LRRTM2 has no direct SCZ literature.
- Recent chandelier-specific snRNA-seq: [Hoftman et al. AJP 2026](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.20250355) explicitly resolves chandelier from PV basket.

**Verdicts.** Synaptic/gated-channel ↓: **Known** (Lewis lab decades). KCNC1 ↓: **Known/Partial** (Kv3.1 literature; not previously chandelier-specific). VGF ↓: **Known** (cross-disorder). **mTORC1 ↑: Partial/Contradicts** — bulk evidence is for mTOR-down; chandelier-specific up could reconcile via cell-type heterogeneity. LRRTM2 ↑: **Novel for chandelier**. FBLN7, MICAL2, KLHL5, ADCY10: **Novel.**

**Coverage caveats.** Most Lewis-lab cartridge work predates snRNA-seq.

### Sst Chodl (long-range projecting NOS1+ GABA)

**Top findings.** 0 genes pass padj<0.1. Top DOWN pathways: GOBP_NEGATIVE_REGULATION_OF_SYNAPTIC_TRANSMISSION (padj=0.031), GOCC_POSTSYNAPSE. Top UP: RNA localization, cytokine signaling.

**Prior literature.**
- Sst_Chodl SCZ literature is **extremely sparse** (cells are ~0.001% of cortical neurons). Most directly relevant: [Loh et al. 2025 Neuron](https://www.cell.com/neuron/abstract/S0896-6273(25)00663-4) 15q13.3 microdeletion mouse — *largest* gene-expression alterations in Sst_Chodl, hyperactivity, SCZ-GWAS-enriched DEGs.
- Broader SST literature: [Joshi 2015 npj Schizophrenia](https://www.nature.com/articles/npjschz20144); [Fung 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379548/); [Volk 2023 JAMA Psych](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2808723).
- [Hoftman AJP 2026](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.20250355) distinguishes Sst_Chodl as discrete subtype in DLPFC.

**Verdicts.** Synaptic transmission ↓: **Known** at broader SST class; **Novel** at Sst_Chodl-specific level. Cytokine signaling ↑: **Partial** (Trépanier 2016 broader neuroinflammation). RNA localization ↑: **Novel.** Top genes (ENOX1, MAP3K13, SPATA6L, DNAH6): **Novel** (no SCZ literature found).

**Coverage caveats.** Essentially no direct human SCZ postmortem literature for Sst_Chodl. Loh 2025 mouse work is closest analog.

### Pax6 (CGE-derived rare interneuron)

**Top findings.** 5/7161 genes pass padj<0.1. Top DOWN pathways: GOMF_GATED_CHANNEL_ACTIVITY (padj=0.0054). Top UP: male gamete generation, TF activity, cytokine binding.

**Prior literature.**
- No direct human postmortem SCZ literature on Pax6 interneuron subclass. PAX6 itself studied as developmental TF ([Manuel 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26304102/); [Cocas 2011](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/31/14/5313)).
- CGE-derived interneurons broadly under-explored in SCZ atlases (Ruzicka, Batiuk).
- Down-regulated gated channels broadly: [Smolin 2012 IJNP](https://academic.oup.com/ijnp/article/15/7/869/638379).

**Verdicts.** Gated channel ↓: **Known** (general SCZ pattern), **Novel for Pax6**. Male gamete UP: **Novel**, likely artifact. Top genes (COL16A1, EPM2A, PTBP1, TSHZ1, CXXC4): **Novel.**

**Coverage caveats.** Pax6 interneurons essentially unstudied in human SCZ postmortem.

### L5 ET (extratelencephalic / pyramidal-tract)

**Top findings.** 3/4162 genes pass padj<0.1. Top DOWN: PDE4B, MFN1. Top UP: ALDH3A2. Pathways weak (lowest padj ~0.19).

**Prior literature.**
- L5 ET scarce in human cortex vs rodent ([Berg et al. 2021 Neuron](https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(21)00627-9?uuid=uuid:38e27516-023c-4551-924c-44710dd89772)).
- L5_6 FEZF2 family in [Batiuk 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) (proportional increase in SCZ DLPFC).
- **PDE4B is one of the most-replicated SCZ genes**: [Millar et al. 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16293762/) (DISC1-PDE4B interaction); [Fatemi 2008](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920996408000923); [meta-analysis Genes 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4425/15/5/609).
- MFN1 / mitochondrial fusion: SCZ DLPFC mito-pathway signal ([Roberts 2021 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7810242/); [Hjelm 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8195258/)).
- L5 dendritic-spine deficits: [Glausier & Lewis 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22546337/).

**Verdicts.** PDE4B ↓: **Known** (replicates Millar/Fatemi, here localized to L5 ET). MFN1 ↓: **Partial/Known** — fits mito-dysfunction in L5 pyramidal. Presynapse ↓: **Partial**. ALDH3A2 ↑: **Novel**. Other loci: **Novel.**

**Coverage caveats.** Underpowered (only 3 padj<0.1 hits).

### L5_6 NP (near-projecting)

**Top findings.** 15/9789 genes pass padj<0.1. Top DOWN: BICDL1, GPC1, CDK9, RAB26, TRPM2. Top UP: RAB3IP, VWA3A, PAPOLA, ZNF345, HOOK1. **Top DOWN pathway: REACTOME_CHOLESTEROL_BIOSYNTHESIS (padj=0.005), GOBP_STEROL_BIOSYNTHETIC_PROCESS.**

**Prior literature.**
- NP-subclass SCZ literature absent — defined recently in Allen / SEA-AD taxonomies.
- Cholesterol in SCZ: [Petrov 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10175956/); [cholesterol synthesis review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7184320/). Antipsychotics modulate SREBP-driven lipogenic transcription including HMGCR/DHCR7 ([Fernø 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1635424/)) — **note: antipsychotics typically UPregulate cholesterol synthesis; our DOWN direction suggests disease-not-drug origin.**

**Coverage caveats.** No NP-specific SCZ literature exists. Cholesterol signal is the most publishable; cross-check against medication status advisable.

## Excitatory neurons (well-studied layers)

### Cross-cutting themes for excitatory

**(A) Cholesterol biosynthesis suppression in L5 IT + L6 IT (and L5_6 NP).** Closest published precedent is [Ling 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5) describing the SNAP (Synaptic Neuron-and-Astrocyte Program) where astrocyte cholesterol biosynthesis (HMGCR, DHCR7, DHCR24, FDFT1, FDPS) declines in lockstep with neuronal synaptic gene expression. **Critically, Ling 2024 maps the cholesterol component to astrocytes**, with Extended Data Figure 7 explicitly showing cholesterol-biosynthesis enrichment is astrocyte-specific. The neuronal "SNAP-n" component captures synaptic genes, not cholesterol. Therefore the L5/L6 IT cholesterol-down signal is **novel as a neuron-intrinsic finding** — fits the SREBF1/SREBF2 GWAS ([Le Hellard 2010](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp2008110); [Steen 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0924977X16301213)) and the [clozapine-induced cholesterol biosynthesis UPregulation in iPSC neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33497908/) (Kruse 2021) — making a disease-DOWN signal stand opposite to the medication effect (i.e. unlikely to be drug-explained).

**(B) Protein folding / chaperone / MYC-targets / mTORC1 UP in upper-layer excitatory.** Partially congruent with [Arion et al. 2007 Biol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/) (HSPA1A/B, HSPB1 chaperone-up in DLPFC SCZ); [Kim 2021 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-019-0537-7) (UPR dysregulation). But contradicts [Chadha 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-020-0614-2) and [Ibarra-Lecue 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105616/) (bulk DLPFC mTOR/S6 DOWN). Reconciliation: cell-autonomous proteostatic stress / compensatory translation response in supragranular pyramidal neurons, distinct from bulk mTOR-S6 axis.

**Verdicts.** Translation ↑: **Partial-to-Novel** for SCZ specifically. PSTPIP2 / FCGR1A activation markers: **Partial** (van Kesteren). GPCR / motility ↓: aligns with loss of homeostatic surveillance ([Snijders 2021](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002%2Fglia.23962)).

## Cross-cutting synthesis

**Themes that replicate strongly across cell types and prior literature:**

1. **Synaptic / neurotransmission DOWN** is pan-cell-type and broadly Known — Batiuk 2022, Ruzicka 2024, Glausier/Lewis, decades of LCM work. Our meta extends to glia (Oligo, Astro, OPC) where the interpretation is mechanistically novel (myelin-axon coupling for Oligo; SNAP for Astro; OPC-axon synapses for OPC).
2. **Cholesterol biosynthesis DOWN** in excitatory L5/L6 IT + macroglia (Astro, Oligo) — **novel at neuron-intrinsic level**, but the SREBF1/2 GWAS + QKI-SREBP2 myelin axis + Ling 2024 SNAP astrocyte component all converge on the same biology. Strong publishable finding.
3. **BMP / SMAD1 UP** across inhibitory (Sst, Pvalb, Vip, Sncg) + Astro — partially anchored by Bayes-Orive 2024 PV mechanism + Reble 2022 bulk; novel as pan-inhibitory pattern.
4. **OxPhos / mitochondrial DOWN in inhibitory** (Sst > Vip > Lamp5 > Pvalb) — Known interneuron OxPhos deficit (Ni 2019 iPSC, Chung 2018 PV LCM), **novel cell-type ordering** that contradicts simple PV-vulnerability model.

**Themes that contradict prior literature:**

1. **mTORC1 UP in Chandelier and OPC** — bulk literature (Chadha 2020, Ibarra-Lecue 2020) shows mTOR/S6 DOWN. Reconcilable as cell-type heterogeneity: a few minority cell types ramp up mTORC1 (Chandelier, OPC, L6 IT) while bulk pyramidal-dominated signal goes down.
2. **Microglia translation UP (NES = 3.17)** — Snijders 2021 reported homeostatic-marker LOSS without activation. Our meta resolves a clear activation signature that smaller cohorts may have missed.
3. **L5/L6 IT cholesterol DOWN** — opposite to clozapine-induced iPSC neuron UP. Argues for disease-not-drug origin.

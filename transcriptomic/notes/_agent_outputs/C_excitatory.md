# Systematic Literature Review: 7 Excitatory Cell Types (Agent C — L2_3 IT, L4 IT, L5 IT, L6 IT, L6 CT, L6 IT Car3, L6b)

## Cross-cutting themes flagged for the cell-type sections

Before diving into individual sections, two cross-cutting themes from the meta-analysis deserve framing because they recur in multiple cell types:

**(A) Cholesterol biosynthesis suppression in L5 IT + L6 IT (and reportedly Astro + Oligo across this meta).** The closest published precedent is [Ling et al. 2024 Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5), which describes the "Synaptic Neuron-Astrocyte Program" (SNAP) — a coordinated transcriptional program where astrocytes express cholesterol biosynthesis genes (including SREBP2 targets, *HMGCR*, *DHCR7*, *DHCR24*, *FDFT1*, *FDPS*) in lockstep with neuronal synaptic-component expression. SNAP declines in SCZ. **Crucially, in Ling 2024 the cholesterol component is mapped to astrocytes specifically**, with Extended Data Figure 7 explicitly showing cholesterol-biosynthesis enrichment is astrocyte-specific. The neuronal "SNAP-n" component captures synaptic genes, not the cholesterol arm. Therefore the L5 IT / L6 IT cholesterol-down signal in our meta is genuinely **novel as a neuron-intrinsic finding** — though it fits the broader SNAP paradigm and the [SREBF1/SREBF2 genetic association](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18936756/) ([Le Hellard 2010 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp2008110)) and [clozapine-induced cholesterol biosynthesis induction in Ngn2 excitatory iPSC neurons](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7987755/) ([Kruse 2021 Schiz Res](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33497908/)). If antipsychotic-naive cases drove the signal it would be especially novel; otherwise it could partly reflect medication-related cholesterol perturbation in the opposite direction (clozapine *induces* cholesterol biosynthesis, so a suppression signal would not be drug-explained).

**(B) Protein folding / chaperone / MYC-targets / mTORC1 UP in upper-layer excitatory.** This is partially congruent with the long-standing observation by [Arion et al. 2007 Biol Psychiatry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/) that prefrontal cortex in SCZ shows up-regulation of chaperones (HSPA1A, HSPA1B, HSPB1) and immune transcripts. Also consistent with the [UPR dysregulation paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7113111/) ([Kim 2021 Mol Psychiatry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-019-0537-7)). On the other hand, mTORC1/translation has historically been reported as **down** in SCZ ([Chadha 2020 Neuropsychopharm](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-020-0614-2); [Ibarra-Lecue 2020 ribosomal S6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105616/)), so the meta-analysis finding of MYC targets / mTORC1 / chaperone UP in upper-layer excitatory neurons is best read as **partial confirmation of the chronic chaperone-up signature, but a contradiction or new wrinkle on mTORC1**. A plausible reconciliation: a compensatory proteostatic stress response in superficial pyramidal neurons, distinct from mTORC1-S6 axis pathology.

## L2_3 IT (701/15879 DE genes; padj<0.1)

**Top findings.** Down: *VWA5B2, PRDM10, ST6GAL2, CDC25B, SOHLH1*. Up: *ADAMTS9-AS2, MAP3K20, SLC5A8, PAIP2B, SIX4*. Pathways: down neuron-to-neuron synapse + cell morphogenesis + vocalization; up protein folding + chaperone + REACTOME cell-cycle-mitotic.

**Prior literature.** Layer 2/3 IT (supragranular) neurons are the **most studied excitatory population in SCZ** and the cell type with the most consistent prior signal in single-cell atlases. The down-regulation of synaptic and morphogenesis programs in L2/3 IT directly mirrors [Batiuk et al. 2022 Sci Adv](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367), who reported the most transcriptomic perturbation in **upper-layer principal neurons** with reduced GABAergic abundance and increased principal-neuron abundance; and [Ruzicka et al. 2024 Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) where L2/3 IT carried the largest number of DE transcripts and most genetic risk. Reduction of dendritic / synaptic complexity in supragranular pyramidal neurons is one of the oldest neuropathological findings in SCZ — see [Glausier & Lewis 2013](http://behavioralhealth2000.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Dendritic-Spine-Pathology-in-Schizophrenia.pdf), [Wong et al. 2016 Transl Psych](https://www.nature.com/articles/tp201643), and a recent [selective vulnerability preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.11.17.386458v2.full) (Bendl et al.).

The up-regulation of **chaperones / protein folding** is convergent with [Arion et al. 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/) (HSPA1A/B, HSPB1 upregulated in DLPFC bulk RNA from SCZ) and [Kim 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31578497/) (UPR dysregulation, increased BiP, sXBP1).

The **cell-cycle-mitotic UP** signal is novel and intriguing. Schizophrenia iPSC-derived NPCs show [aberrant G2/M cell-cycle control](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11526604/), but neuronal cell-cycle re-entry signatures in postmortem SCZ have not been a major theme. *CDC25B* DOWN combined with REACTOME cell-cycle-mitotic UP is paradoxical and worth probing — CDC25B is a G2/M phosphatase; downregulation could reflect post-mitotic stabilization while broader cell-cycle gene up-regulation may capture cyclin/CDK/E2F transcripts independent of CDC25B.

Specific genes: *ST6GAL2* DOWN dovetails with [Mueller 2018 glycosylation enzyme dysregulation](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/369314.full.pdf) in elderly SCZ DLPFC. *ADAMTS9-AS2* is not previously named in SCZ literature; lncRNAs broadly are dysregulated ([emerging lncRNA review PMC9548578](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9548578/)).

**Verdicts.** Synapse/morphogenesis DOWN: **Known** (Batiuk 2022, Ruzicka 2024, Glausier/Lewis). Chaperone UP: **Known** (Arion 2007, Kim 2021). Cell-cycle-mitotic UP: **Novel** in postmortem snRNA-seq. *ST6GAL2* DOWN: **Partial** (general glycosylation dysregulation prior). *ADAMTS9-AS2*, *PRDM10*, *SOHLH1*, *VWA5B2*, *MAP3K20*, *PAIP2B*, *SIX4*: **Novel** as individual loci.

**Coverage caveats.** Ruzicka 2024 supplementary tables would resolve per-gene Known/Novel verdicts; not freely accessible without journal access.

## L4 IT (509/14335 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *HES4, RPS6KL1, BICDL1, ATP2B2, VWA5B2*. Up: *LGI1, NUCB2, SMAD1, MXD1, TOB1*. Pathways: down glycoprotein metabolic + organic anion transport + active transmembrane transporter; up HALLMARK_MYC_TARGETS_V1, ribonucleoprotein granule, REACTOME ESR-mediated signaling.

**Prior literature.** L4 IT (granular/stellate-like in DLPFC, more clearly stellate in sensory cortices) is a critical SCZ population because [SCZ common-variant risk enriches specifically in mature L4 glutamatergic neurons emerging in infancy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11295574/) ([Skene 2018 / Ling et al. 2024 lineage analysis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11295574/)). The early stellate-neuron literature reported reductions in GluR3, NMDAR1, synaptophysin, SNAP25 in this population ([Mirnics et al. 2000 Neuron](https://www.cell.com/fulltext/S0896-6273(00)00085-4), [Pierri 1999](https://www.nature.com/articles/4001319)).

The **MYC TARGETS V1 UP** in L4 IT fits a documented "ribosome subunits upregulated in a subgroup of SCZ" pattern from a [systematic gene-expression meta-analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022395623003114) and dovetails with chaperone/translation responses cited above. **REACTOME ESR-mediated signaling UP** — estrogen receptor signaling has been recurrently invoked in SCZ but rarely cell-type-mapped to L4 IT specifically (mostly studies focus on systemic estrogen and overall cortical effects).

*LGI1* UP: notable; [LGI1 variants are associated with SCZ via the NgR1–LGI1 axis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4947863/) (Karadima 2008; Loh 2016). *ATP2B2* DOWN is consistent with prior reports of [PMCA2 perturbation and risperidone response association](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/22/6/2785). *HES4* DOWN points to attenuated Notch signaling, congruent with [Hoseth 2018 Sci Rep on attenuated Notch in SCZ + BD](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-23703-w).

**Verdicts.** Synaptic/transporter DOWN: **Known** (Mirnics, Pierri). MYC targets UP / RNP granule UP: **Partial** (meta-analytic ribosome up-regulation reported, not specifically in L4 IT). ESR signaling UP: **Partial / Novel** for L4-IT specificity. *LGI1* UP: **Known** as risk locus but direction in L4 IT is **Novel**. *ATP2B2* DOWN: **Known**. *HES4* DOWN (Notch): **Known direction**, **Novel locus**. *RPS6KL1, BICDL1, VWA5B2, NUCB2, SMAD1, MXD1, TOB1*: **Novel**.

**Coverage caveats.** Limited cell-type resolution in older bulk LCM work makes direct L4 comparison imperfect.

## L5 IT (805/13908 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *RCSD1, LNX1, BICDL1, GASK1B, ETS2*. Up: *LINC00158, CXXC4, TTC29, DUBR, NUCB2*. Pathways: **down REACTOME_CHOLESTEROL_BIOSYNTHESIS (NES=-2.43)**, NOTCH binding, response-to-immobilization-stress; up male gamete generation, mRNA 3' end processing, kidney development.

**Prior literature.** L5 IT in DLPFC is the deeper IT pyramidal layer, well-studied via [Arion 2015 LCM transcriptomics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4492919/) and [Arion 2017 BD/MDD-distinguishing analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5610065/). Arion's group reported reduced energy metabolism / mitochondrial transcripts (COX4I1) and altered synaptic genes in L5 pyramidal cells in SCZ ([Arion 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0006322317314427)).

**The cholesterol biosynthesis DOWN signal in L5 IT is the most striking novelty of this meta.** As detailed in the cross-cutting section above, [Ling 2024 Nature SNAP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10954558/) maps the cholesterol arm to **astrocytes**, not excitatory neurons. The L5 IT neuron-intrinsic cholesterol-down signal is, to our knowledge, **not previously reported in this cell type in postmortem SCZ snRNA-seq.** It is consistent with the [SREBF2 GWAS locus (chr22q13.2)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0924977X16301213) and with the converging cholesterol theme from [oligodendrocyte/Qki-SREBP2-cholesterol axis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8139834/). Notably, it is **opposite in direction** to the [clozapine-induced cholesterol biosynthesis upregulation in iPSC neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33497908/) (Kruse 2021), strengthening the case it is disease-, not drug-, driven.

The down NOTCH binding in L5 IT echoes the broader Notch attenuation signal ([Hoseth 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-23703-w)). The up "male gamete generation" / kidney pathways are likely **artifactual / non-canonical hits** from MSigDB — common with preranked GSEA across loosely overlapping gene sets — but worth checking leading-edge gene composition (could reflect cilia/microtubule/transport machinery shared between sperm flagella and neuronal projections).

*ETS2* DOWN is congruent with [ETS2 over-expression inducing neuronal apoptosis](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8956222) — direction matches a "protective downregulation". *LINC00158* and *DUBR* UP: novel lncRNA findings.

**Verdicts.** Cholesterol biosynthesis DOWN: **Novel as a neuron-intrinsic finding** (Known as theme via Ling SNAP astrocyte component / SREBF2 GWAS / clozapine pharmacology). NOTCH DOWN: **Partial / Known direction**. Synaptic/mitochondrial DOWN signal (implicit in synaptic-context genes): **Known** (Arion 2015/2017). Male gamete / kidney UP: likely **GSEA-artifactual**; worth manual leading-edge inspection. *RCSD1, LNX1, GASK1B, TTC29, CXXC4*: **Novel** loci.

**Coverage caveats.** Ling 2024 did not deeply explore deep-layer IT specifically for cholesterol; targeted re-analysis of their snRNA-seq for L5/L6 IT-specific cholesterol gene scores would directly test this verdict.

## L6 IT (133/11174 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *MAFK, SYT12, ZFP64, HTR4, ADGRB1*. Up: *SEMA6D, GAB1, KIF13A, LMCD1-AS1, SMAD1*. Pathways: **down REACTOME_CHOLESTEROL_BIOSYNTHESIS (NES=-2.35)**, sterol biosynthetic, isoprenoid biosynthetic; up cytoplasmic translation, HALLMARK_MYC_TARGETS_V1, REACTOME cellular response to starvation.

**Prior literature.** L6 IT — a deep intratelencephalic population — is **less well-characterized than L2/3** in SCZ literature. Most "infragranular" work pooled L5+L6 ([Arion 2010 infragranular gene expression](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2823856/) showed disturbed neurodevelopmental gene expression in pooled IG SCZ samples).

The **same cholesterol/sterol/isoprenoid biosynthesis DOWN signature** as L5 IT — extremely strong corroboration that this is a real, deep-IT-specific neuron-intrinsic effect, mirrored across two related cell types. Same novelty verdict as L5 IT applies. Notably, the **isoprenoid biosynthetic process DOWN** is mechanistically informative: isoprenoids are required for protein prenylation (small-GTPase membrane targeting) and dolichol synthesis, suggesting downstream effects on Rho/Ras signaling and N-glycosylation — consistent with the [glycoprotein metabolic process DOWN](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/369314.full.pdf) seen in other cell types.

The **MYC TARGETS / cytoplasmic translation / starvation response UP** in L6 IT is congruent with the [ribosome subunits up in SCZ subgroup meta](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022395623003114). However, it appears **contradictory to the [downregulated AKT-mTOR pathway in DLPFC postmortem SCZ](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-020-0614-2)** (Chadha 2020) and [ribosomal S6 hypofunction reported in SCZ](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105616/) (Ibarra-Lecue 2020). One interpretation: a cell-autonomous translational stress / amino acid deprivation response (REACTOME cellular response to starvation UP) in deep IT neurons specifically, distinct from bulk AKT-mTOR signaling.

*SYT12* DOWN: aligns with broader [synaptotagmin/syntaxin downregulation in SCZ superior temporal gyrus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3464383/). *HTR4* DOWN: serotonin receptor with [haplotype association to Japanese SCZ](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0022799); direction in L6 IT is novel. *SEMA6D* UP: SEMA6D is a [SCZ GWAS susceptibility gene with pleiotropic psychiatric/metabolic effects](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S016517811500534X) — finding it dysregulated in L6 IT is biologically consistent.

**Verdicts.** Cholesterol/sterol/isoprenoid DOWN: **Novel** for L6 IT specifically (Known theme). MYC targets / cytoplasmic translation UP: **Partial / Contradicts** (Known direction in subgroup ribosome meta, contradicts overall mTOR downregulation). *SYT12, HTR4* DOWN: **Partial / Known direction**. *SEMA6D* UP: **Partial** (Known SCZ risk locus). *MAFK, ZFP64, ADGRB1, GAB1, KIF13A, LMCD1-AS1, SMAD1*: **Novel**.

**Coverage caveats.** L6 IT is rarely separated from L5 IT in older bulk LCM work; some "infragranular" findings may apply.

## L6 CT (107/9693 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *RXFP1, SYT12, WSB1, EFHD2, TM7SF3*. Up: *MSC-AS1, GHR, SUGT1, ZFHX4, CDC42EP3*. Pathways: down platelet homeostasis, metal ion transport, neurotransmitter transport; up GOBP protein folding, REACTOME HSF1 activation, chaperone-mediated protein folding.

**Prior literature.** L6 CT (corticothalamic) neurons are a critical population for thalamocortical loop function and a **major focus of SCZ circuit literature** ([untangling cortico-thalamo-cortical loop review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9006917/); [cortico-thalamic development in SCZ review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroanatomy/articles/10.3389/fnana.2023.1130797/full); [22q11.2 thalamic mechanisms review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8693383/)). [L6 CT neurons drive high gamma oscillations](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.10.05.616808v1.full) — a known SCZ phenotype. However **molecular-level findings on L6 CT in postmortem SCZ snRNA-seq are sparse** — Ruzicka 2024 and Bast 2025 enumerate L6 CT but neither makes it a major story.

The **HSF1 activation / chaperone / protein folding UP signature in L6 CT is striking and convergent with [Arion 2007 chaperone upregulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/)** (HSPA1A/B, HSPB1) and consistent with [HSF1's documented protective neuronal role](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/34/5/1599). Worth interpreting as a **cell-type-specific concentration of the cortical proteostatic stress response in corticothalamic neurons** — biologically plausible because of high metabolic demand of long-range projection neurons.

*RXFP1* DOWN: relaxin family peptide receptor with limited prior SCZ literature; expressed in cortical neurons and implicated in cognition. **Novel**. *SYT12* DOWN: same pattern as L6 IT (see above). *WSB1, EFHD2, TM7SF3* DOWN: novel. *GHR* UP (growth hormone receptor) and *ZFHX4* UP: novel; ZFHX4 is a developmental transcription factor.

The **platelet homeostasis DOWN** pathway is curious in a CNS context and likely reflects shared platelet/neuron molecular components (e.g., serotonin signaling, calcium handling) — typical MSigDB cross-tissue artifact requiring leading-edge inspection.

**Verdicts.** HSF1 / chaperone UP: **Partial / Known direction** for the cortex broadly (Arion 2007), **Novel for L6 CT specifically**. Protein folding UP: **Known** (Arion 2007, Kim 2021 UPR). Neurotransmitter transport DOWN: **Known** (decades of SCZ literature on glutamate/GABA transporters; e.g., [EAAT2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38825587/)). *RXFP1, SYT12, WSB1, EFHD2, TM7SF3, MSC-AS1, GHR, SUGT1, ZFHX4, CDC42EP3*: largely **Novel** as L6 CT-specific findings.

**Coverage caveats.** L6 CT is rarely isolated in older laminar LCM work; the [PsychENCODE Phase II](https://www.psychencode.org/phase-ii) cell-type-specific results may overlap but were not directly queryable here.

## L6 IT Car3 (84/9400 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *NTNG1 (estimate=-0.54, padj=8.3e-12 — by far the strongest hit in this cell type), RGS12, MAML3, NFIB, CELF4*. Up: *ZHX2, PARP8, ARHGAP5, MLLT3, DPY19L3*. Pathways: down GOCC synaptic membrane (padj=2e-08), postsynaptic membrane, cell projection morphogenesis; up male gamete generation, gamete generation, sexual reproduction.

**Prior literature.** L6 IT Car3 is a **claustrum-projecting / claustrum-like population** ([Bakken 2021 mouse atlas](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Great-ape-specialization-of-L6-IT-Car3-neurons-A-UMAPs-of-L6-IT-Car-neurons-in-each_fig1_363677223); [Wang 2025 spatial atlas of claustro-insular region](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63138-2)). Importantly, **a [recent integrative analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9569514/) reported a decrease in the proportion of L5/6_IT_CAR3 cells in SCZ patients** (Ji 2022). The cell type has been highlighted for "great-ape specialization" of its transcriptomic identity, making it a candidate substrate for cognitive specializations relevant to psychiatric illness.

***NTNG1*** **DOWN is the most striking single-gene finding in the meta-analysis** (padj 8.3e-12). NTNG1 (netrin-G1) has the **most direct, replicated prior SCZ literature of any gene in this review**: [Aoki-Suzuki et al. 2005 Biol Psychiatry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15705354/) reported family-based association of NTNG1 haplotypes with SCZ and altered mRNA isoform expression in postmortem SCZ brain; [Eastwood & Harrison 2008 Neuropsychopharmacology](https://www.nature.com/articles/1301457) reported decreased NTNG1 mRNA in temporal lobe SCZ + BD; NTNG1 is preferentially expressed by excitatory neurons. Our finding in L6 IT Car3 specifies the **cell-type substrate** of a long-known SCZ DEG. This is a **Known direction with Novel cell-type specificity** — high-confidence biologically.

The **synaptic membrane / postsynaptic membrane DOWN** with strong padj is consistent with [Batiuk 2022](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abn8367) and [Ruzicka 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adg5136) themes and the [Lewis dendritic spine literature](http://behavioralhealth2000.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Dendritic-Spine-Pathology-in-Schizophrenia.pdf).

The **male gamete / sexual reproduction UP** pathways are again likely MSigDB cross-tissue artifacts driven by cilia/microtubule shared genes (LMCD1 family, dyneins) — common GSEA pitfall, requires leading-edge inspection.

*RGS12* DOWN: RGS family genes (notably RGS4) have a long SCZ history ([Mirnics 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11532720/)); RGS12 specifically is novel-direction. *NFIB* DOWN, *MAML3* DOWN, *CELF4* DOWN, *ZHX2* UP: **Novel** as SCZ-specific loci.

**Verdicts.** *NTNG1* DOWN: **Known** (Aoki-Suzuki 2005, Eastwood/Harrison 2008) — strong validation of the meta-analysis pipeline; novel cell-type specificity. Synaptic membrane DOWN: **Known**. Cell-type proportion / vulnerability concept: **Known** (Ji 2022 proportion decrease). *RGS12, MAML3, NFIB, CELF4, ZHX2, PARP8, ARHGAP5, MLLT3, DPY19L3*: **Novel**. Male gamete UP: likely **GSEA artifact**.

**Coverage caveats.** L6 IT Car3 is the most novel transcriptomic category here (only recently named); pre-2020 literature does not address it directly.

## L6b (256/9501 DE genes)

**Top findings.** Down: *SLC39A11, OLFML2B, VWA5B2, CHKA, PKNOX2*. Up: *FAM161A, GNAS, MXD1, FCHO2, PCBP3*. Pathways: down hydrolase activity on glycosyl bonds, hydrolase O-glycosyl, glycoprotein metabolic; up REACTOME ESR-mediated signaling, REACTOME innate immune system, REACTOME attenuation phase.

**Prior literature.** L6b is the **adult cortical remnant of the embryonic subplate**. [Hoerder-Suabedissen et al. 2013 PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3587197/) (gene expression profiling of mouse subplate) explicitly **linked subplate gene networks to autism and schizophrenia genetic risk** — a foundational paper establishing L6b as SCZ-relevant. However, **direct molecular findings in adult postmortem SCZ L6b are very sparse** — most SCZ snRNA-seq atlases either pool L6b with L6 CT or report few DE genes due to low cell counts.

The **glycoprotein/glycosyl hydrolase DOWN signature** in L6b dovetails with the [Mueller 2018 glycosylation enzyme dysregulation paper](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/369314.full.pdf) in elderly SCZ DLPFC and the [galactose/GalNAc transferase paper](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/649996.full.pdf).

The **REACTOME innate immune system UP** is consistent with the long-running [Arion 2007 immune/chaperone upregulation theme](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/) and with [C4 / complement upregulation in SCZ](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9564272/). REACTOME attenuation phase (HSF1 deactivation phase) UP is consistent with the chaperone-up signature seen in other layers.

*GNAS* UP: [GNAS (Gαs) is genetically linked to SCZ](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3312743/) and overexpression in mouse cortex/striatum produces SCZ-like phenotypes (Kelly 2008); finding it UP in L6b is congruent. *CHKA* DOWN: links to phosphatidylcholine biosynthesis; reduced CHKA could compromise membrane phospholipid synthesis and contribute to membrane phospholipid abnormalities long noted in SCZ ([Horrobin classic literature]); no prior direct SCZ-CHKA report at cell-type level. *MXD1* UP: a MYC antagonist; counterintuitive if cytoplasmic translation/MYC-targets are also up elsewhere. **PKNOX2, SLC39A11, OLFML2B, FAM161A, FCHO2, PCBP3, VWA5B2**: novel loci.

**Verdicts.** Glycoprotein/glycosyl hydrolase DOWN: **Partial / Known direction** (Mueller 2018). Innate immune / chaperone-attenuation UP: **Known direction** (Arion 2007, Sekar 2016 C4), **Novel for L6b**. *GNAS* UP: **Known direction** (Kelly 2008), **Novel for L6b specificity**. *CHKA* DOWN: **Novel direction** at cell-type level; consistent with longstanding membrane-phospholipid theory. L6b vulnerability concept: **Known** (Hoerder-Suabedissen 2013). Individual loci (*SLC39A11, OLFML2B, VWA5B2, PKNOX2, FAM161A, MXD1, FCHO2, PCBP3*): **Novel**.

**Coverage caveats.** L6b is the smallest cell population in most snRNA-seq atlases (~2-5% of cortical nuclei); cross-cohort meta-analysis power may explain why findings emerge here that are absent from individual cohort studies. The [Hoerder-Suabedissen 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3587197/) work is on mouse subplate development, not adult human L6b, so adult-stage validation is largely missing from the literature.

## Cross-cell-type integration from this agent

1. **Cholesterol biosynthesis suppression in L5 IT + L6 IT (and Astro/Oligo per your meta)**: The clearest precedent is [Ling 2024 Nature SNAP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07109-5), which mapped this signature to **astrocytes**, not neurons. The neuron-intrinsic deep-IT signal here is a **Novel extension of the SNAP framework**, consistent with the [SREBF1/SREBF2 GWAS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0924977X16301213) and [Qki-SREBP2-cholesterol myelinogenesis axis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8139834/), and opposite to [clozapine-induced upregulation in iPSC neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33497908/) (suggesting disease-not-drug origin).

2. **MYC targets / mTORC1 / chaperone UP in upper-layer excitatory**: **Partially confirms** [Arion 2007 chaperone upregulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2080683/) and the [ribosome-up-in-SCZ-subgroup meta](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022395623003114). **Contradicts** [Chadha 2020 AKT-mTOR downregulation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-020-0614-2) and [Ibarra-Lecue ribosomal S6 hypofunction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105616/) at the bulk level — reconciled best as a **cell-autonomous proteostatic stress / compensatory translation response in supragranular pyramidal neurons**, distinct from bulk-tissue mTOR-S6 down.

3. **Coverage caveats overall**: Ruzicka 2024 Science paper text is paywalled (403 from Science.org); supplementary tables would be necessary for exhaustive gene-level Known/Novel adjudication. Bast 2025 medRxiv is a preprint. Hoerder-Suabedissen 2013 is mouse-developmental. Several gene-level "Novel" verdicts may flip to "Partial" if Ruzicka 2024 supp tables are consulted.

**Fig. 5 | Shared and depletion-graded transcriptional dysregulation of Sst interneurons in schizophrenia.**
(**a**) Definition of the three depletion groups. Per-supertype abundance change in SCZ (crumblr estimate ± SE, from Fig. 3a) against median cortical depth measured in Xenium (0 = pia), for the 16 Sst supertypes. Depleted, FDR < 0.20 (n = 5 supertypes); intermediate, negative but non-significant (n = 6); non-depleted, estimate ≥ 0 (n = 5). Dashed lines, the FDR < 0.20 boundary and zero; right gutter, cortical layer boundaries drawn from the Xenium sections. Spearman ρ (abundance change, cortical depth) = 0.74.
(**b**) Gene sets significantly enriched in each group, by direction and FDR tier; bar labels give the FDR < 0.10 count. "All Sst" is the pooled subclass analysis of Fig. 2.
(**c**) Per-gene meta-analytic z (estimate/SE; SCZ versus control) in the depleted versus the non-depleted group, for the 9,631 genes tested in both. Points are coloured by membership of the leading-edge union of the gene sets in d; dashed line, identity; ρ, Spearman. Labelled genes are shown in bold in e.
(**d**) Normalized enrichment scores for representative gene sets, grouped into blocks shared across the subclass (synaptic, ubiquitin) and blocks graded by depletion (cytosolic translation, oxidative phosphorylation).
(**e**) Group-level meta-analytic z for exemplar leading-edge genes of each block. *SST* is shown for reference and is not a leading-edge member of the plotted synaptic sets.
Throughout, differential expression is one pseudobulk per donor per group (donors with ≥ 10 nuclei: n = 395, 411 and 342 for the depleted, intermediate and non-depleted groups), meta-analysed across the seven snRNA-seq datasets as in Fig. 2 and followed by preranked gene-set enrichment (Methods). Significance throughout: \*\*\*FDR < 0.01, \*\*FDR < 0.05, \*FDR < 0.10.
